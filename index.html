Final-day tradeoff · Saturday relocation option
Keeping Saturday as a full Smokies hiking day at the same camp leaves a long Sunday return. Three writing hours, a 9 PM bedtime and an entirely scenic 400+ mile final ride do not all fit comfortably.

Default: keep both hiking days and the three two-night bases; Sunday gets a scenic start and substantial I-81 travel. Check a live ETA before departure.

Optional change: make Monday your second 4–7 mi hiking day at Cumberland Gap, keep Wednesday's Big South Fork hike, and replace Saturday's Smokies hike with a northeast move toward Bristol / Abingdon. Camp at a confirmed low-elevation site with showers, then use selected US-11 / Appalachian back roads on Sunday. This gives five camp setups and a more relaxed scenic finish.

Do not combine a full Saturday hike with a late, long relocation. This alternative is not included in the default colored route or budget.

Camping, showers & connectivity

Oct 4–5 · Wilderness Road: non-electric $18/night, picnic table, water and showers. Plan two offline writing mornings; recharge before arrival or ask about an electric site ($24/night). Camp details / booking.

Oct 6–7 · Bandy Creek: showers, tables and water; budget $25/night as an allowance, confirm the current tent-site checkout price. Write offline at camp. Visitor-center Wi-Fi is a daytime option, not guaranteed early-morning access. Camp details / booking.

Oct 8 · Indian Boundary: candidate near Cherohala; allow $30, confirm October availability, facilities and exact site fee before committing. It is a higher plateau camp, not the lowest-elevation option. If the overnight forecast is cold, ask about a lower Tellico Plains tent campground instead. Check Indian Boundary.

Oct 9–10 · Townsend: Little River Campground is a candidate offering tent sites, bathhouse access and advertised Wi-Fi. Ask for the cheapest tent rate and electricity / charging access; $40/night here is a placeholder allowance, not a quote. Check tent sites.

Connectivity mix: two Townsend nights with advertised Wi-Fi, two Bandy Creek nights near daytime visitor-center connectivity, and three nights primarily offline. A third night with dependable Wi-Fi would require a confirmed Tellico alternative. No campsite Wi-Fi quality is guaranteed.

Shower rhythm: Sun or Mon at Wilderness Road; Wed at Bandy Creek; Fri and/or Sat at Townsend. No gym membership needed. If choosing a cheaper Smokies camp without showers, Little River advertises a $10 shower pass for non-guests; confirm office hours. Compare campsite + shower costs together.

Sleep: favor valley camps; ride the ridges during the day. Check sleeping-bag comfort rating and pad insulation against the overnight low, since a three-season tent alone does not provide warmth. Confirm quiet surroundings when choosing a site.

Hikes, backups & conditions

Wed core: Twin Arches Loop, about 4½ mi, moderate, allow 3–4 hours on foot. Trailhead transfer can involve unpaved access; confirm surface conditions and avoid a muddy approach. Backup: John Litton Farm Loop, 6.3 mi from Bandy Creek, or Angel Falls Rapid, 4 mi round trip.

Sat core: Abrams Falls, 5 mi round trip. NPS's August 2026 construction notice says closed Monday–Thursday and open Friday–Sunday, so Oct 10 fits the advertised pattern; recheck before riding to the trailhead. Backup: Chestnut Top, 5.6 mi round trip. Cades Cove traffic can consume hours; choose one hike.

You plan to ride through rain. Reduce the day's optional mileage in poor visibility; do not count rain as a reason to hurry. A forecast or closure can change the optional route each morning.

For Smokies trailhead parking longer than 15 minutes, buy/display the required parking tag: $5 daily / $15 weekly. Includes motorcycles. Download trail maps and navigation before departure.
